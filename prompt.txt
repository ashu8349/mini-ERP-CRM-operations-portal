==================================================
10. CHALLAN BUSINESS LOGIC
==================================================

This is extremely important.

When a challan is saved as DRAFT:

- Do NOT reduce stock.

When a challan is CONFIRMED:

- Validate stock for every product.
- If any product has insufficient stock:
    - Reject the entire operation.
    - Return proper HTTP 400 error.
    - Do NOT partially update stock.
    - Do NOT confirm the challan.

If stock is sufficient:

For every challan item:

currentStock -= quantity

Create:

StockMovement:
movementType = OUT
reason = "Sales Challan"

Perform all stock updates and challan confirmation inside ONE PostgreSQL transaction.

Example:

Product A stock = 20
Challan quantity = 5

After confirmation:

Product A stock = 15

Stock movement:

Product A
Quantity = 5
Type = OUT
Reason = Sales Challan
Created By = logged-in user

==================================================
11. CHALLAN STATUS RULES
==================================================

DRAFT:
- Can be edited
- Does not affect stock

CONFIRMED:
- Stock has been reduced
- Cannot be edited

CANCELLED:
- Should not reduce stock if cancelled before confirmation

Do not allow invalid status transitions.

For example:

CONFIRMED -> DRAFT

must not be allowed.
